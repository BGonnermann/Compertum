The alignment defect belongs to this private prototype. The original scanner already supplies aligned buffers. Windows requires8-byte alignment for [FILE_FULL_DIR_INFORMATION](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows-hardware/drivers/ddi/ntifs/ns-ntifs-_file_full_dir_information). The final prototype adds `_Alignas(8)` and an `offsetof` static assertion. The subsequent matching inventories support that targeted correction; they do not qualify the complete scanner.

The final timeout includes cold DLL initialization, synchronous callback/deadline checks, Python batch merging, link validation and checked closure. Its partial count and category totals do not establish a complete inventory or current capacity. No controlled whole-tree performance comparison succeeded, so this result does not demonstrate an improvement over the published reader. The earlier profiles identified directory acquisition as the largest observed cost, but this candidate did not separately time those operations. No antivirus, power, disk or scheduler cause was established.

All eight native investigation Jobs are closed and report zero remaining children. Seven have `DIAGNOSTIC_CLOSED` with observed exit0. The original round11 comparison has a preserved `FAILED` receipt with process exit observed; its child exit code was not saved. The later diagnosis explains the Python refusal but does not replace that missing historical field. Wrapper exit0 on a diagnostic refusal means recording and cleanup succeeded; it is not scan acceptance.

Fresh experiment scratch retained a128MiB namespace ceiling and separately enforced1MiB per-job growth. No installation, spending, service, security/power/priority change, Strata startup, broker action or deletion occurred. Both complete source/installed environment qualifications and product tests were deliberately not launched after timing failed.
