## Change and limits

`calculate_position_costs` validates inputs with the unchanged original reconciliation calculator.
It then compares costs that do not depend on an unverified broker cost method.
Purchases before a partial sale have exact aggregate cost.
Uniform-price partial sales retain exact remaining cost.
Mixed-price partial sales and later different-price purchases remain unsupported.
That history stays unsupported after complete sale, reopening or a change of date.
An unsupported symbol cannot hide another symbol's incorrect cost.

Supported costs must match the reported cost. Position quantity and average price must also agree.
Results keep false financial and transport authority. Current evidence, native provenance, fees and actions remain unqualified.
The existing private original-record context does not yet use this new calculation.

Alpaca documents different intraday and overnight cost methods.
This calculation supports only histories with directly determined costs.
See [Alpaca's cost methods](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/position-average-entry-price-calculation), checked 16 October 2026.
