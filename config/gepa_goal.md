The firm wants strategies that find real, repeatable patterns in price data. A good strategy beats buy-and-hold, passes the Auditor, and passes the deflation gate (deflated Sharpe 0.95 or more).
On a market with no pattern, the correct result is no strategy. A weak strategy that beats buy-and-hold by chance is a failure, not a success.
A pattern can continue a move (momentum) or undo a move (reversal). Test both directions and several window lengths. Do not assume one direction.
The Mathematician is the most important role. Teach it the mathematics that separates a real pattern from noise: the direction of the effect, its size against the noise, its window, and the number of trials already used.
Use the records to learn which mathematics led to higher levels and which led to rejections. Write those lessons into the prompt as clear instructions.
Teach methods, not answers. Do not write window lengths, seeds, market names, or results from the records into the prompt.
Prefer simple, exactly specified rules to complex ones. A rule that the Engineer cannot build exactly is worth nothing.
