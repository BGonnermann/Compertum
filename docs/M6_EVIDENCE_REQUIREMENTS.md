# M6 evidence checker requirements

Independent bounded audit,11 October2026. BUILD_SPEC sections14-17 and docs/M5_M6_PREPARATION.md govern. This is a plan for the authorized offline checker, not actual M6 acceptance. Paper consumption integration is independently reviewed and undergoing final paired verification.

Each immutable session must bind project/account alias/private context, unique session/date, pinned XNYS calendar, actual operating interval, code/environment/config hashes and preceding-session reference. Bind exact input/action/quote/signal/model/protocol identities and eligibility/audit references. Preserve legitimate no-order decisions.

Supervision evidence must include native owner identities/generations, ordered heartbeat records, acknowledgements and arm/disarm transitions. Compute coverage from records; supplied healthy booleans are insufficient. Bind complete intent/authorization/risk/attempt/broker/fill/ledger ranges, retaining uncertainty, cumulative fills, fee/action/settlement explanations and unexplained discrepancies.

Closing evidence requires fresh complete account/order/position reconciliation and halted/disarmed admission. Daily flatness and daily trading are not requirements; explain and link legitimate holdings. Recovery needs an exact validated RecoveryManifest/artifact closure plus actual restore evidence: generation transition, preserved consumed attempts, invalidated arms, safe message delivery once, no broker-write replay, and reconciliation after restore. Manifest validation alone does not establish an actual restoration. AI-off evidence must bind actual intervals, disabled configuration, invocation history and continuing deterministic/risk/monitoring operation.

Use bounded artifact references with identity, SHA256 and size, verifying exact bytes, sequence/count boundaries and transitive references. Missing/truncated evidence cannot count as complete. Retain public aliases/opaque references and protected private identity. Reuse data/calendar.py session_times, strict canonical paper JSON, existing recovery lineage/closure validators and immutable record patterns. Historical read evidence does not regain freshness or authority.

Aggregate exactly ten consecutive completed XNYS sessions using exchange successors, including holidays/early closes/DST. Reject duplicate dates/IDs, missing exchange days and future/uncompleted intervals. Preparation-only, DETERMINISTIC_FAKE, PAPER_WIRE_FIXTURE, accelerated clocks, backtests and historical replays contribute zero actual sessions. Discrepancies break qualification and remain visible. Keep252prospective strategy sessions and profitability/strategy acceptance separate.

Unspecified acceptance predicates must remain UNVERIFIED until frozen:
1. Minimum operating interval that qualifies a supervised day.
2. Whether recovery and AI-off exercises must occur within the chosen ten-session window.

Smallest implementation: one bounded immutable checker module and focused reference/chronology/consecutive-session/provenance tests. Separate structural fixture validation from actual evidence qualification. No extra owner, journal, scheduler, transport or actual account access. Native actual-session producers and actual M5 evidence remain prerequisites for M6 acceptance.
