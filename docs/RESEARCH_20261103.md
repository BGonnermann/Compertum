## 1. Microsoft R&D-Agent(Q)

Owner: "yes run a sub agent on R&D-Agent". Sources: [paper v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.15155v2), [repository](https://github.com/microsoft/RD-Agent), [AlphaAgent](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.16789v1), [Profit Mirage](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.07920).
- Design (read): a loop of specification, synthesis, implementation (the Co-STEER code agent, at most 10 repairs per task), validation and analysis.
  A linear Thompson-sampling bandit chooses factor work or model work. A knowledge base stores (task, code, feedback) and retrieves similar tasks.
  A new factor is dropped when its daily correlation with a kept factor is 0.99 or more. An LLM decides yes or no on keeping a result.
- Results (read): CSI 300 test 2017 to 2020, 14.21% a year and IR 1.74, against 5.70% for Alpha158. Bandit ablation: 8.97% random, 10.09% LLM choice, 14.21% bandit (single runs).
- Evidence is weak (inferred): the default backtest window appears to be the test segment that also feeds the keep decision; no multiple-testing correction; the main test is before the models' cutoff dates.
- Owner decision, 3 November 2026: build the code library for the Engineer (idea 1) and the Director's step-type information (idea 2, "Information only": a table of step types, tries and average outcome; the Director still chooses).

## 3. Raising the deflated Sharpe ratio honestly

Owner: "find more strategies to bring our deflated sharpe up without overfitting or falsifying information".
- The DSR depends almost only on the trial count N and the t-statistic of active returns. At N = 28,315 a stage-1 DSR of 0.95 needs an IR (annual active Sharpe) of about 1.04 on french49 VALIDATION.
  Skewness and kurtosis barely matter, because the daily Sharpe ratio is about 0.03.
- Stage 1 only ranks. The real pass is the vault, where N counts vault candidates: observed vault IR of about 0.37 at N = 1, 0.55 at N = 3 and 0.73 at N = 12 (inferred).
- xs_momentum is at about IR 0.48 on VALIDATION (inferred from the sweep report); its vault IR may be lower after publication decay (McLean and Pontiff; the exact figure is not verified).
- Ideas, each tested with knobs chosen on TRAIN and every setting counted: turnover bands (Novy-Marx and Velikov), a full-breadth tilt blended with equal weight (Clarke, de Silva and Thorley),
  volatility scaling of the tilt (Barroso and Santa-Clara; counter-evidence Cederburg et al.), equal-risk blends of weak, low-correlated signals (value, seasonality, factor momentum).
- Owner decisions listed (each "changes the judge, gate or trial count"): vault order and batch size, effective number of trials, an investable benchmark, a walk-forward test from 1936, pre-registration.
- Dishonest ways to avoid: dropping or resetting trials, tuning on VALIDATION, chosen sub-periods, a lower minimum, retesting changed copies of failed candidates.
- Top steps: one pre-registered "momentum done well" library family (sealed `library_multi.py`, owner decision); a first vault batch of one candidate; decade and Newey-West diagnostics; French factor data.
