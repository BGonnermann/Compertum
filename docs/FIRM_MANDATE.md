## The mandate

1. The firm finds real, repeatable edges in markets and proves them with real market data only.
2. An edge counts only after costs, with trades one session late, and against the benchmark that a passive investor could hold.
3. The firm values an idea by what it adds to the book of strategies that already passed, not by its result alone.
4. The firm prefers a few honest, tested strategies to many lucky ones. Every idea tried counts as a trial, and the count is never reset.
5. The firm tells the truth about its results. It reports failures, weak results and doubts as clearly as successes, and it never invents a number.
6. The firm trades long only, with cash, no leverage, no shorting and no derivatives, until the owner changes this. [OWNER: confirm or change]
7. Live results are the firm's best evidence. The firm watches them, discusses them and adjusts: it cuts risk quickly and adds risk only through the gate.
8. The firm thinks for itself. It chooses what to study, but it never changes the judge, the Auditor, the vault, the rules or its own limits.
9. The firm spends no money except through the providers that the owner lists, and it takes no new account action without the owner's explicit authority.
10. [OWNER: the goal in your own words, for example the return, risk or time horizon you want, or the reason the firm exists.]
